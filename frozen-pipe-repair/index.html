Local Cause

Why Certain Fort Collins Pipes Freeze

Pipes running through unheated crawlspaces, exterior walls, or garages are the most common freeze points, especially in older homes in Fort Collins' historic neighborhoods where insulation standards from decades ago don't match what's expected today. A single hard cold snap, not necessarily an extended deep freeze, is often enough to freeze an exposed line that's marginal to begin with. The two problems are related closely enough that Burst Pipe Repair is worth a look too.

Diagnosis

How We Handle a Freeze Call Safely

A technician locates the frozen section first, often by process of elimination based on which fixtures are affected, then applies controlled, safe thawing methods rather than an open flame, which can damage pipe or start a fire. If the pipe has already split, the priority shifts immediately to shutoff and repair rather than thawing. Either way, the visit ends with a look at what made that section vulnerable in the first place, since a freeze rarely happens in a spot that was already well insulated.

Preparing Exposed Pipes Before the Next Cold Snap

Preparing Exposed Pipes Before the Next Cold Snap

Fort Collins winters don't always give much warning before a hard freeze, so it's worth walking your home's crawlspace, garage, and exterior walls once each fall to identify any exposed pipe runs. Foam insulation sleeves are inexpensive and take minutes to install on an accessible section. For known problem areas that have frozen before, a low-wattage heat cable is a more permanent fix than relying on insulation or a dripping faucet every time temperatures drop. If the cause turns out to be broader than expected, Sump Pump Installation & Repair is usually the next relevant page.

FAQs

Common Questions

Is it safe to thaw a pipe myself with a hair dryer?

Gentle, gradual heat can be reasonably safe for an accessible section, but never use an open flame, and call us if you're unsure or can't reach the affected area.

How do I prevent this next winter?

Insulating exposed pipe runs in crawlspaces, garages, and exterior walls is the most effective prevention, and we can identify at-risk spots during a repair visit.

Can a frozen pipe burst even without a hard freeze?

Yes, even a single sharp cold snap can freeze and split a marginal, poorly insulated line.
