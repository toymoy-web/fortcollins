Hydro Jetting in Fort Collins, CO

When a drain clears with a snake but clogs again within weeks, hydro jetting is usually the next real step before considering a pipe replacement. Fort Collins 24/7 Plumbing uses high-pressure water jetting to fully clear grease buildup and minor root intrusion that a mechanical snake only punches a hole through. Why Snaking Alone Sometimes Isn't Enough

A snake pokes a hole through a clog and lets water flow again, but it doesn't remove the grease film coating the inside of the pipe or the root mass that caused the blockage in the first place. In Fort Collins' older neighborhoods with mature street trees and aging clay sewer lines, that means the same clog often returns within a matter of weeks. Hydro jetting scours the full diameter of the pipe rather than just opening a channel through the blockage.

Diagnosis

How We Confirm Hydro Jetting Is the Right Call

A camera inspection runs first in nearly every hydro jetting case, since jetting an already-fragile or cracked pipe can cause damage rather than fix the problem. Once the camera confirms the line can handle it, jetting clears the full pipe wall using controlled water pressure calibrated to the pipe's material and condition. The whole process is typically finished in the same visit as the camera inspection, so there's rarely a need to schedule a separate appointment once the line's condition is confirmed.

Spacing Out Future Jetting Visits

Spacing Out Future Jetting Visits

Once a line has been fully jetted, staying ahead of the next clog is mostly about what goes down the drain afterward. Kitchens that pour grease down the sink regularly will see buildup return faster than one that doesn't, regardless of how thoroughly the line was cleaned. For lines with a history of root intrusion, a repeat camera check every year or two is a reasonable way to catch regrowth before it causes another backup, since roots can return to an already-compromised joint even after jetting clears them out.

FAQs

Common Questions

Is hydro jetting safe for older pipes?

It depends on the pipe's condition, which is why we run a camera inspection first before jetting anything.

How long does hydro jetting take?

Most residential jobs take one to two hours depending on line length and buildup severity.

Will this stop tree roots from coming back?

Jetting clears existing root growth, but roots can regrow over time near an already-compromised joint. We'll flag if a line looks like a repeat-jetting candidate.

Is hydro jetting messy or disruptive?

It's done through existing cleanouts in almost every case, so there's minimal disruption inside the home itself.
