Local Cause

Why Main Water Lines Fail Here

Aging pipe material, particularly older galvanized or original copper service lines, is the most common cause of main line failure in Fort Collins' established neighborhoods. Ground movement from Colorado's freeze-thaw cycle can also stress a service line over time, especially at the point where it enters the home's foundation. A fair number of these calls end up touching Repiping & Whole-Home Pipe Replacement too, so we look at both together.

Diagnosis

How We Diagnose a Main Line Problem

Locating equipment traces the exact path and depth of the buried service line first, since guessing at a main line's location risks damaging other utilities. Leak detection equipment then narrows down the specific point of failure along that path before any digging happens. Utility locating is a standard, non-negotiable step on every main line call, since a mis-located dig risks striking gas or electrical lines that share the same general path.

Options

Spot Repair vs. Full Line Replacement

Spot Repair

  • Limited digging at the confirmed leak point
  • Lower cost than full replacement
  • Right call for a single, isolated failure
  • Requires the rest of the line to be sound

Full Line Replacement

  • Needed for widespread corrosion or repeated failures
  • Trenchless options available depending on layout
  • Higher upfront cost
  • Resolves ongoing pressure or leak issues for good

Watching for Early Main Line Trouble

Watching for Early Main Line Trouble

A gradual drop in whole-house water pressure over several months, rather than a sudden change, is often the earliest sign of a main line problem developing. It's easy to write off as normal aging, but worth mentioning to a technician during any other plumbing visit. Keeping an eye on your yard for a consistently wet or unusually green patch, even without an obvious leak, can also catch a slow main line leak before your water bill reflects it. This is worth reading alongside Leak Detection, since the two issues share a lot of the same root causes.

FAQs

Common Questions

Is the main water line my responsibility or the city's?

Generally, the portion from the meter to your home is the homeowner's responsibility. We'll confirm the specifics for your property.

Can you avoid digging up my driveway?

Trenchless methods can sometimes avoid disturbing a driveway or sidewalk, depending on the exact layout. We'll evaluate this during inspection.

How do you find the line without digging test holes everywhere?

Locating equipment traces the buried line's path and depth accurately before any digging starts.

Will I be without water during the repair?

Yes, water service is typically off for the duration of the repair, and we'll give you a realistic timeframe before starting.
