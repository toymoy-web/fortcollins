Local Cause

Common Causes of Sewer Line Damage Here

Root intrusion at pipe joints is the most common cause we see in Fort Collins' older neighborhoods, where clay sewer lines installed decades ago have had plenty of time for roots to find a way in through small gaps. Ground shifting from Colorado's freeze-thaw cycle can also crack or separate joints over time, particularly in older infrastructure that predates modern pipe materials. Any repair or replacement work has to meet the Colorado Plumbing Code's requirements for sewer line material and installation, the state's adopted version of the International Plumbing Code (IPC). This overlaps with Sewer Line Replacement often enough that we check for it on the same visit.

Signs to Watch For

Symptoms We See in Fort Collins

Sewage Backup Into the Lowest Drain

Usually the first sign of a significant main line blockage or break.

Sinkhole or Soft Spot in the Yard

Can indicate a collapsed or badly cracked section of pipe below.

Recurring Clogs Despite Regular Snaking

A sign the pipe itself, not just debris, is the problem.

Sewage Odor Outdoors Near the Line's Path

Often a sign of a leak along the buried line rather than inside the house.

Diagnosis

How We Diagnose Sewer Line Damage

A camera inspection runs the full length of the accessible line to locate the exact point and type of damage, whether that's a root mass, a cracked joint, or a bellied section holding standing water. Locating equipment then marks the exact depth and position from the surface, so any digging that is needed is limited to the actual problem area rather than the whole line. We'll walk you through the camera footage in plain terms before quoting anything, so you understand exactly what's being repaired and why.

Options

Spot Repair vs. Full Line Replacement

Spot Repair

  • Limited digging at the exact damage point
  • Lower cost than full replacement
  • Right call for a contained crack or root intrusion
  • Requires the rest of the line to be in good condition

Full Line Replacement

  • Needed when damage is extensive or the line is very old
  • Higher upfront cost
  • See our sewer line replacement page for details
  • More disruptive but resolves ongoing recurrence

Watching for Early Warning Signs

Watching for Early Warning Signs

Sewer line damage rarely appears overnight. A slow drain that snaking temporarily fixes, a faint sewage smell in the yard, or a slightly soft spot in the lawn are all worth a camera check before they become a full backup. Homes with mature trees near the line's path are worth checking proactively every couple of years even without symptoms, since root intrusion in Fort Collins' older clay lines tends to progress quietly until it doesn't. We regularly see this paired with Hydro Jetting on the same property.

FAQs

Common Questions

Do you have to dig up my whole yard?

Not usually. A spot repair limited to the actual damage point is possible whenever the rest of the line is sound.

How do you find the exact problem without digging first?

A camera inspection combined with locating equipment identifies the exact depth and position before any digging starts.

Is trenchless repair available?

In some cases, yes, depending on the type and location of the damage. We'll confirm after the camera inspection.

Will my landscaping be restored after the repair?

Yes, any disturbed landscaping at the dig site is restored as part of the job once the repair is complete.
