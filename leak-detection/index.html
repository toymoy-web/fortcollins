Where These Leaks Usually Start

A large share of the leak-detection calls we get involve water heaters leaking near the edge of a garage slab, water meters that keep running even when every fixture in the house is off, and slab leaks where warm water is detectable coming up through the floor. Older copper supply lines, common in homes built before PEX became standard, are also more prone to pinhole leaks as they age.

Diagnosis

How Electronic Leak Detection Works

A technician starts by isolating which side of the system the leak is on, checking the water meter with all fixtures off as a first diagnostic step. From there, acoustic listening equipment and, when needed, thermal imaging help pinpoint the leak's actual location without opening walls speculatively. The goal is always to confirm the exact spot before any cutting or digging happens.

Catching a Leak Before the Bill Does

Catching a Leak Before the Bill Does

Checking your water meter with everything off once every few months is a simple habit that catches a developing leak long before it shows up as a shockingly high bill. Homes with older copper supply lines are worth an occasional proactive check even without symptoms, since pinhole leaks in aging copper tend to develop gradually and quietly. If your home has had one slab or hidden leak before, it's reasonable to have the rest of the system checked at the same time, since the same age-related wear affecting one section is often present elsewhere too.

FAQs

Common Questions

Will you have to cut into my walls to find the leak?

Not usually. Electronic detection equipment finds the source first, so cutting is limited to the confirmed spot when repair happens.

How do I know if I have a slab leak?

A warm spot on the floor, an unexplained water bill increase, or the sound of running water are the most common signs. A detection visit will confirm it either way.

Is leak detection covered separately from the repair?

Detection is typically quoted as its own visit, and repair is quoted once the leak is actually located.
