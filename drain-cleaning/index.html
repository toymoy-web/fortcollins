Local Cause

What's Actually Clogging Fort Collins Drains

Root intrusion is a real, recurring cause in Fort Collins' older neighborhoods, where mature street trees have had decades to find their way into clay or cast-iron sewer lines. In newer construction near Windsor and the eastern edge of the service area, clogs are more often grease, wipes, or ordinary buildup rather than roots. A drain that clears with a snake but clogs again within a few weeks is a strong sign of root intrusion or a structural pipe issue, not a one-off blockage. This is worth reading alongside Hydro Jetting, since the two issues share a lot of the same root causes.

Signs to Watch For

Symptoms We See in Fort Collins

Slow Draining Sink or Tub

Usually grease, hair, or soap buildup in the trap or branch line, the easiest category to clear.

Gurgling From Multiple Drains

A sign air is trapped behind a blockage further down the line, often the main sewer line rather than one fixture.

Recurring Clog in the Same Spot

Clears with a snake but comes back within weeks, a classic sign of root intrusion or a pipe defect.

Sewage Smell Near a Floor Drain

Can indicate a main line blockage pushing gas back up through the lowest fixture in the house.

Diagnosis

How We Diagnose a Recurring Clog

A single slow drain usually gets snaked first, since that resolves the majority of calls without further equipment. When a clog recurs, is in the main line, or the caller mentions roots or an older clay sewer line, a camera inspection runs next to see the actual condition of the pipe rather than guessing. Hydro jetting, which uses high-pressure water rather than a mechanical snake, is the standard next step for grease buildup or minor root intrusion that a snake alone won't fully clear.

The Visit

What to Expect During a Drain Cleaning Visit

A single slow drain gets snaked first in nearly every case, since that resolves the clear majority of calls quickly and at the lowest cost. If the clog is in the main line, recurs after clearing, or the camera shows roots or a structural pipe issue, we'll explain exactly what we saw, on the camera footage itself, before recommending hydro jetting or any further work. We never recommend jetting or a larger repair without showing you the actual evidence first, since a snake fixing the visible symptom doesn't always mean the underlying cause is resolved.

Options

Snaking vs. Hydro Jetting

Snaking

  • Fastest, lowest-cost option
  • Clears most single-fixture clogs
  • Doesn't remove root growth or grease film
  • Right first step for most calls

Hydro Jetting

  • Clears grease buildup and minor roots fully
  • Higher cost than snaking alone
  • Recommended for recurring clogs
  • Often paired with a camera inspection first

Keeping Drains Clear Between Visits

Keeping Drains Clear Between Visits

Most recurring drain calls trace back to the same handful of habits: grease poured down a kitchen sink, wipes flushed even when labeled flushable, and hair left to accumulate in a shower drain over months. None of those cause an emergency on their own, but they compound with the root intrusion already common in Fort Collins' older sewer lines, so a line that's marginal from roots clogs faster when it's also carrying grease buildup. If your home has mature trees near the sewer line's path, an annual camera check is a reasonable way to catch root growth before it causes a backup, especially heading into a wet spring. The two problems are related closely enough that Sewer Line Repair is worth a look too.
