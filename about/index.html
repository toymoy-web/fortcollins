About Fort Collins 24/7 Plumbing.

Locally dispatched plumbing service for Fort Collins, Loveland, LaPorte, and Windsor, CO, licensed under Colorado's statewide plumbing board.

Who We Are.

A Locally Dispatched Plumbing Service.

Fort Collins 24/7 Plumbing provides licensed plumbing service across Fort Collins, Loveland, LaPorte, and Windsor, Colorado. Calls are answered locally, in the 970 area code, rather than routed through an out-of-state call center. We work under Colorado's statewide plumbing licensing system, the same standard that applies across Larimer County.

How We Work.

Upfront Pricing, Every Time.

Every job gets a flat price, agreed before any work starts, whether that's a same-day water heater replacement or a full sewer line repair. We diagnose the actual cause of a problem before recommending a fix, and we'll tell you plainly when a repair makes more sense than a replacement, or the other way around. Calls are taken 24 hours a day, every day, including nights, weekends, and holidays.

Licensing.

Colorado DORA State Plumbing Board.

Work performed by Fort Collins 24/7 Plumbing falls under the Colorado DORA State Plumbing Board's licensing system, which applies statewide with no local reciprocity exceptions. If you have questions about license verification, the board maintains public licensee records you're welcome to check independently.

Any permit-requiring work, a repipe, a new gas line, a sewer line replacement, is also performed to the Colorado Plumbing Code, the state's adopted version of the International Plumbing Code. Fort Collins, as a home-rule city, can layer its own local amendments on top of that baseline, which is part of why we confirm the specific requirements for your address rather than assuming a generic statewide answer covers every job.

Why We Work This Way.

Straight Answers Over Upselling.

A lot of plumbing calls have an obvious, inexpensive fix and a more expensive alternative that isn't actually necessary yet. We'd rather tell you honestly when a repair will hold for years than push a replacement you don't need this visit. That approach costs us some short-term revenue on individual calls, but it's also why callers come back the next time something goes wrong instead of calling around for a second opinion.

The same principle applies to diagnosis. We use the actual right tool for the job, a camera for a sewer line, electronic equipment for a hidden leak, a pressure test for a gas line, rather than guessing based on symptoms alone and hoping the first repair attempt happens to be correct right away. That's slower on some calls, but it genuinely means fewer repeat visits for the same underlying problem.

How We Dispatch.

Local Technicians, Not a Call Center.

When you call, a dispatcher based in the 970 area code answers directly, not a national call center reading from a script. That dispatcher's job is to understand what's actually wrong before a technician is sent, since a clear description of the problem, running toilet versus no hot water versus a visible leak, changes which parts and equipment the technician brings on the first visit. Getting that right the first time is a big part of why most calls resolve in a single visit rather than requiring a return trip.

Routes are planned daily across Fort Collins, Loveland, LaPorte, and Windsor, which is why same-day windows are realistic for most calls placed before mid-afternoon. Emergency calls, a burst pipe, a flooded basement, no hot water in winter, get priority over routine scheduled work, and that priority holds at 2 a.m. the same as it does at 2 p.m.
